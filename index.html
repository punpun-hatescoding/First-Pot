    Hand-build

    Add: pinch the clay and pull out, like adding a coil. Press: pinch once to make a dent. Smooth: pinch and rub. With the mouse, press and drag instead.

    Mirror copies one side to the other, so two ears come out even. Zoom with + and −.

    With the camera: hold a hand over a tool to switch (the Mirror chip turns mirroring on and off). Pinch to work, open your hand to stop. Hold a hand at the far left or right edge to turn the clay. Give a thumbs-up and hold it to move on.

    Alter it

    The wheel has stopped. While the clay is still soft you can change it by hand. Pick a tool, then pinch near the pot or press and drag with the mouse.

    With the camera: hold a hand over a tool on the left to switch. Pinch to work, open your hand to let go. Give a thumbs-up and hold it to move on.

    Wedge the clay

    Clay from the bag has air trapped in it. Push the lump away from you and roll it back, again and again, until the holes are gone. Pinch (or press the mouse) and push up, then come back down.

    Air in the clay

    Slow, even pushes work best. Rushing folds air back in. Air left in the clay can burst in the kiln.

    With the camera: no pinch needed. Hold up open hands, push them up and away, then bring them back down. Repeat. Give a thumbs-up and hold it when you're done.

    Add-ons

    Small clay shapes, stuck on while the piece is still leather-hard. Pick a shape, then touch the piece where it should go. Or skip this step.

    Paint it

    It has been fired once and is dry and pale now. Paint on it with underglaze colours, or print a picture on it. The glaze goes over the top in the next step.

    Centre the clay

    Bring your hands to the clay until it glows where you touch, then hold them still. The wobble settles.

    Centred

    Throw

    Touch the clay, then move in to narrow it and lift it, or out, slowly, to swell it. Move up and down to choose where.

    Whole hand shapes the pot. Fingertip presses a groove at the height you touch: hold longer for a deeper one, then move up or down for the next. Rows of grooves make ridges. Go over a groove with the whole hand to smooth it away.

    When it feels finished, give a thumbs-up and hold it for a moment. A thumbs-up hand never touches the clay. No need to click. With a keyboard, press Enter.

    It fell

    Everyone's first one does. You can keep this one exactly as it is, or start again. Nothing is wasted: the clay goes into the reclaim bucket and comes back, marbled, in your next piece.

    Choose a glaze

    The pot has dried and been fired once. Now pick what it will be dipped in. You will not know exactly how it turns out.

    Hold a hand over a glaze bucket on the wheel until its ring fills.

    How the kiln cools decides the surface. Cooled slowly, the glaze goes soft and matte. Cooled fast, it stays glassy. Choose this first, then the glaze.
